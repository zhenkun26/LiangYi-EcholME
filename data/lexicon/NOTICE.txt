The bundled echolme-lexicon.idx is derived from the pinned CC-CEDICT export
dated 2026-10-11T08:29:27Z. Derivation keeps one simplified-Chinese subtitle and
separate English-word and English-phrase indexes. It does not relicense the
data under the GPL. See data/lexicon/manifest.json for counts, checksums, and
selection rules. The rime-essay scores used while building the index are an
existing vendored word-frequency prior, not a second translation dictionary.
